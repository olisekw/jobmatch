# IDEA.md

I'm a software developer, and I built this for myself because job hunting was wasting my time in
a specific way: I'd keep running into postings I'd already seen, or already applied to and never
heard back from, with no record of any of it. If a listing wants a JavaScript developer and I have
JavaScript on my CV, that's a real signal — I've done something with it — and I wanted a tool that
could act on that signal automatically instead of me re-reading the same boards by hand.

So the idea was: paste in your CV, it pulls out your skills, and every job gets scored against
them — not just on skills, but on seniority, on whether you want remote, hybrid or on-site, and on
location and pay. Then you browse by score instead of by chance, and once you apply, the tool
remembers it, so you stop wasting time on jobs you've already tried. I wanted it to cover
international postings too, not just local ones, since that's what I was actually applying to —
which is also where I hit the first real wall: LinkedIn requires a logged-in session to scrape,
so it was never going to be one of the boards this covers.
